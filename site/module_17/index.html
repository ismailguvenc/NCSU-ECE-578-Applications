Accuracy and pedagogical standards

Editorial rules

Questions are worded with explicit LTE release and mathematical assumptions when necessary. The revised original bank is traceable to the source and correct asterisks. New questions are labeled separately.

Explanations emphasize why an answer is correct, rather than only stating a letter. “Incorrect statement” stems may be less pedagogically transparent than direct concept questions; both formats are included to facilitate comparison.

The initial QA pass corrected a number of known ambiguities but is not a replacement for a faculty approval pass or comprehensive 3GPP conformance audit of every historic item. Quiz answers are intentionally stored in the JavaScript bundle, so this is not an anti-cheating examination tool.
